Budget ownership should sit with user's own leaders, prevents the "us vs you mentality". 
As an internal platform engineer your job should be to provide and guide on what budget controls exist, not deciding which levers to pull

Budgets should be CLEAR and well monitored, no user should be caught off guard when they run out of tokens (reminders, reports, easy look ups)

Modifications, top ups, etc. should be intuitive and efficient, self service or some operationalized process
